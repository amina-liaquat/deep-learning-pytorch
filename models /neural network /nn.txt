NEURAL NETWORK

Neural Network as a mathematical system inspired by the human brain. Just as your brain has interconnected neurons that fire signals to process information, 
an artificial neural network has "nodes" (neurons) that process data to find patterns.

LAYERS AND NEURONS

A standard neural network is organized into three main types of layers:

-Input Layer: This is where the raw data enters the system (e.g., the pixels of an image or the words in a sentence).

-Hidden Layers: This is where the "thinking" happens. A network can have one or hundreds of these. They extract features—the first layer might find edges, the second   might find shapes, and the third might find eyes or ears.

-Output Layer: This provides the final answer or prediction.
